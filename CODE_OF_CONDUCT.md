# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and focused on the technical work.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different levels of experience and domain expertise.
- Give actionable technical feedback.
- Prioritize patient-data safety, reproducibility, and research integrity.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information or credentials.
- Uploading private clinical data or restricted datasets.
- Misrepresenting model or benchmark results.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate these
standards. Serious or repeated violations may lead to a ban from project spaces.

Report issues through the security or maintainer contact path listed in `SECURITY.md`.
